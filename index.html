Dost, tujhe janamdin ki dher saari badhaiyaan. Tu meri best friend kam, meri khud ki family zyada hai — jisko main bina soche sab kuch bata deta hoon, chahe baat kitni bhi bewakoofana kyun na ho.

Humne kabhi is dosti ko naam nahi diya, phir bhi duniya ki sabse solid team hain hum. Tu mujhe seedha karti hai jab main bigadta hoon, aur main tujhe jhelta hoon jab tu drama karti hai. Yehi hamara deal hai.

Is saal bas itna maangta hoon — tu jitni pagal hai utni hi khush rahe, aur teri hasi kabhi kam na ho, chahe meri jokes kitni bhi bakwas kyun na ho.

Bas yaad rakhna — kuch bhi ho jaaye, sabse pehle main khada milunga.
